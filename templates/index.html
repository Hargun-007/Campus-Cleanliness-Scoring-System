⚙️ Methodology & Technical Pipeline

Transparent, explainable computer vision architecture designed for campus maintenance

Object Detection Model
YOLOv8n (Nano)
Transfer learning with fine-tuned single-class head (litter) on TACO benchmark.
Item Count Penalty
1 - e-0.35 · N
Exponential decay penalizing high frequencies of discrete litter items.
Area Footprint Ratio
min(1, Area / 0.15)
Normalizes total bounding box pixel area against overall image dimensions.
Cleanliness Score
0 – 100 Scale
Balanced combination: 50% item count penalty + 50% area coverage penalty.
